# Wuxi Vision Pro (无锡蕾明视康科技有限公司)

> Wuxi Vision Pro Co., Ltd. (蕾明视康), founded in 2010, is a Chinese ophthalmic
> medical device company that develops, manufactures, and sells intraocular
> lenses (IOLs) and other high-end ophthalmic consumables. The company focuses
> on foldable intraocular lens R&D and industrialization, building mid- to
> high-end IOL product lines to substitute imported products in the Chinese
> market. Headquartered in Wuxi (Binhu District) with a branch office in
> Shanghai. Backed by Qiming Venture Partners.

Wuxi Vision Pro is a medical device manufacturer, not an API provider. No
public developer portal, API documentation, machine-readable API artifacts,
client packages, or GitHub organization were found for this company. Its
website (https://wx-visionpro.com) is served from a China-based CDN that
returns HTTP 429 to requests from outside China.
